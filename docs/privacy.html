🐦 BlueBird DocuCam

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

BlueBird DocuCam is a document-camera app for macOS, built to be private by design. The short version: we don’t collect anything, and your camera never leaves your device.

We don’t collect your data

BlueBird DocuCam does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising.

Your camera stays on your device

The app accesses your camera only to show and capture documents on your Mac. Video and images are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Snapshots and PDFs you save are written only to the location you choose.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase and any free trial are handled by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details. Apple’s handling of that transaction is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

No third-party sharing

Because we don’t collect anything, we have nothing to sell or share with anyone.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
