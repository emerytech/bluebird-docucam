🐦 BlueBird DocuCam

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

BlueBird DocuCam is a document-camera app for macOS, built to be private by design. Your camera stays on your device.

Local app data

BlueBird DocuCam has no accounts or advertising. Camera settings, classroom presets, and first-lesson checklist progress are stored locally on your Mac and are not uploaded to us.

Optional installation count in direct downloads

On macOS, Windows and Linux, “Include this install in the active count” is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings under Privacy. While enabled, DocuCam sends a random installation ID to our Cloudflare service about once a day while running. We store the ID and its most recent UTC date to count installations active over 30 days. Older entries are cleared daily. Turn the checkbox off to stop future check-ins. The App Store edition does not send check-ins.

Your camera stays on your device

The app accesses your camera only to show and capture documents on your Mac. Video and images are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Snapshots and PDFs you save are written only to the location you choose.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase and any free trial are handled by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details. Apple’s handling of that transaction is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Optional trial reminders and feedback

If you enable a trial reminder, DocuCam schedules a local notification using the trial end date reported by Apple. You can turn it off in the app or manage notification permission in System Settings. No email address is required.

If you choose to share feedback, DocuCam opens an email draft with your selected reason. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send that email, we receive its contents and the sender information provided by your email app so we can respond and improve DocuCam. Please avoid including student information or sensitive documents.

No third-party sharing

We do not sell your information or share your optional feedback for advertising.

Children’s privacy

Optional feedback should be sent by an adult and should not include student information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
