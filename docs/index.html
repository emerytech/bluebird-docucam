iPhone as the camera

Continuity Camera turns your iPhone or iPad into a sharp document camera. Any USB camera works too.

⏺

Full-screen & live

Crisp edge-to-edge projection that auto-connects when you plug a camera in.

✎

Freeze & annotate

Pause a frame and draw on it with pen or highlighter, then wipe clean and go live.

📄

Scan to PDF

Capture page after page and save the whole set as one clean PDF.

🔍

Zoom, rotate, flip

Zoom in on the fine print and fix upside-down or mirrored text in a keystroke.

📷

Snapshot

Grab any frame to an image file or the clipboard for your notes and handouts.
