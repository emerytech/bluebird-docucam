The Water Cycle

Water is always on the move. The sun warms lakes and oceans until the water turns to vapor and rises into the sky — this is evaporation.

High up, the vapor cools and gathers into clouds. We call this condensation. When the droplets grow heavy enough, they fall as rain or snow — precipitation.

The water flows into streams and rivers, back to the sea, and the whole cycle begins again.
